## 1. Objective and scope

Record an instrumented Qwen3.5-35B-A3B IQ2_XXS reasoning-battery run using **Tandem.cpp** and the **Nepenthes** methodology. The battery contains ten sequential cases: three arithmetic questions, five `two_sum` exercises, and two `binary_search` exercises, with varying requested budgets and generation limits.

This is an independent run, not a controlled A/B comparison with `cafe-llama.cpp`. Engine, cache, execution-order, and system-state differences prevent a direct comparison.

## 8. Limitations and conclusions

1. All ten requests ran, but four outputs were truncated by their generation limits.
2. Functional quality was assessed by static inspection; generated Python fragments and their assertions were not executed.
3. The reasoning budget was submitted by the client, but server-side enforcement has not been demonstrated.
4. Prompt-cache state varies substantially; prefill rates are not directly comparable across all cases.
5. Resource aggregates use per-request means and sample counts vary.
6. No model SHA-256 or complete server log is included.

**Conclusion:** this run now has complete, auditable primary evidence for all ten cases. Aggregate generation throughput was 32.30 tok/s (total tokens / total generation time), with a 33.90 tok/s unweighted per-request mean. The runner completed 10/10, but only six responses ended with `stop`; four were truncated. Static inspection considers five complete responses correct and flags a minor test-comment defect in the sixth. No automated functional validation or overall battery pass is claimed.
